ABOUT THE WEBSITE

About Site

ASHIQ TRADLINK is a product catalogue for local shopkeepers to choose wholesale boxes, review quantities and totals, and prepare an order message for WhatsApp.

Minimum goods order: ₹500. Delivery is calculated at ₹4 per one-way kilometre from Mangupur. The customer checks the order and sends it through WhatsApp.
